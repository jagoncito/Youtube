TITLE
Beethoven: Symphony No. 1 | Jagoncito’s Cozy Village Evening

DESCRIPTION
Beethoven’s complete First Symphony accompanies Jagoncito and a little hedgehog beneath a stone arch, after the rain.

This new digital orchestral realization includes all four movements, with the score’s internal repeats and Menuetto return. Expect lyrical passages, lively rhythms and strong changes in energy throughout. The complete programme plays once; no movement is cut or repeated to fill a target duration.

The village scene and thumbnail were created for this video. Small drips, puddle ripples and fern leaves move gently; the animals remain still. The soundtrack contains only the orchestra.
