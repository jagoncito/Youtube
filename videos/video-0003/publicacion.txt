TÍTULO
Quiet Focus in Jagoncito’s Workshop | Satie Piano & Rain

DESCRIPCIÓN
Clay bowls and an open sketchbook rest on a sheltered workbench above a rain-soaked forest. Jagoncito has found a quiet corner while a kitten sleeps beneath the stool.

Soft Satie piano for quiet focus, accompanied by a very light layer of rain. Delicate rain moves outdoors beyond the pottery workshop.

Erik Satie’s Gymnopédies No. 2 and 1, played completely in a newly rendered digital interpretation. This 30-minute session repeats a six-minute musical programme five times.
