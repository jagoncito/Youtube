DESCRIPTION
A moonlit cove, a distant lighthouse and a little warmth beside the sea. Jagoncito and a kitten rest on their blanket as silver reflections reach across the water.

Debussy’s Clair de lune, from Suite bergamasque, accompanies the scene in a new digital grand-piano interpretation. Its delicate opening grows into flowing, more expressive passages before returning to stillness.

The complete 5:27 performance is repeated 6 times for a 32:43 session. Every repeat uses the same newly rendered interpretation, with its full ending and measured natural decay preserved. No music is cut, and the tempo has not been changed to reach a fixed video length.

This coastal scene was newly created for this video, with gently moving sea, surf and reflections. Jagoncito, the kitten and the lighthouse remain still. The soundtrack contains only the piano performance, with no sea or wind sound effects.
