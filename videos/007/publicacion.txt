DESCRIPTION
Morning light reaches across the vineyard as Jagoncito watches an autumn leaf from a stone step. A tiny mouse waits beside the harvest basket, with copper-coloured vines and a village on the hills beyond.

Beethoven’s Adagio cantabile accompanies this autumn morning in a new digital grand-piano interpretation. Its lyrical main theme gives way to more expressive episodes before returning. This is the complete second movement of the Pathétique Sonata, not the full three-movement sonata.

The complete 4:47 performance is repeated 6 times for a 28:40 session. Each cycle uses the same newly rendered interpretation, with the full ending and natural piano decay preserved. No music is cut, and the tempo has not been changed to fit a fixed video length.

The vineyard scene and thumbnail were newly created for this video. A few vine leaves move gently, with occasional dry leaves drifting across the scene. Jagoncito, the mouse and the leaf on the step remain still. The soundtrack contains only the piano performance, with no outdoor sound effects.
