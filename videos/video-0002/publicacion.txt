TÍTULO
Unwind with Jagoncito | Gentle Satie Piano on a Rainy Night

DESCRIPCIÓN
A warm window seat shelters Jagoncito, a sleepy kitten and a tiny mouse from the rainy forest outside.

Soft Satie piano for winding down, accompanied by a very light layer of rain. Gentle rain, warm firelight and very subtle kitten breathing accompany the piano.

Erik Satie’s Gymnopédies No. 1 and 2, played completely in a newly rendered digital interpretation. This 30-minute session repeats a six-minute musical programme five times.
