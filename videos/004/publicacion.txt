TITLE
Vivaldi’s Winter | A Snowy Refuge with Jagoncito

DESCRIPTION
Vivaldi’s Winter unfolds outside Jagoncito’s snowy mountain refuge: shivering strings, a lyrical Largo and the restless return of the winter winds. A small dragon and a kitten share the lantern's warmth while snow drifts through the forest.

This new digital realization includes all three movements of the complete concerto, played by sampled solo violin, string sections and quiet organ bass. The music moves between urgency and stillness; its lively outer movements retain their contrast.

The complete 9:11 concerto is repeated three times for a 27:33 session. The repeats use the same newly rendered performance, with its complete endings and natural room tails preserved. No movement is cut, and the tempo has not been changed to reach a fixed video length.
